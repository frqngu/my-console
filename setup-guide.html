Set up your console

  • Do the steps in order. Don't skip ahead.
  • Tick the box next to each step when it is done. Every box must be ticked: when they all are, this window closes by itself.
  • If you get stuck, find the step number and read the red-orange "If it doesn't work" line under it.
  • On your PlayStation-style controller, ✕ means "select" and ○ means "go back".
  • You will need: your store logins (Steam, Epic, Ubisoft), your phone (stores sometimes text you a code), and a keyboard and mouse for the websites.
All done! This window will close in a moment. You can open it again any time from Settings → Games & library → Setup guide.

PART 1: THE CONTROLLER

1.1 Connect it

  1. Plug the small end of the USB cable into the controller.
  2. Plug the big end into the PC.
  3. Wait 10 seconds.

1.2 Test it

  1. Look at the console screen.
  2. Push the left stick left, then right.
  3. ✅ The highlight should move. If it does, go to Part 2.
If it doesn't work: unplug it, wait 10 seconds and plug it back in. Try a different USB port on the back of the PC. Still nothing: stop and tell Frankie.

PART 2: THE WELCOME SCREEN AND YOUR PROFILE

2.1 Get past the first screen

  1. You will see a screen waiting for the PS button.
  2. Press the PS button in the middle of the controller.
  3. ✅ The screen changes to the welcome screen.
If nothing happens: press Enter or Space on the keyboard.

2.2 Make your profile

  1. Follow the questions on screen. Move with the left stick and press ✕ to choose.
  2. For your name, press ✕ on the name box. A keyboard appears on the screen.
  3. Move the stick to each letter and press ✕ to type it. When the name is typed, choose Done or Save.
  4. Pick a picture if it asks.
  5. ✅ You land on the home screen with your name in the top left.

PART 3: SIGN IN TO YOUR GAME STORES

3.1 Open the sign-in screen

  1. On the home screen, push the stick up to the top bar.
  2. Move right to the gear icon (Settings) and press ✕.
  3. Choose Games & library and press ✕.
  4. ✅ You see rows for Steam, Ubisoft Connect and Epic Games, each saying "Sign in".

3.2 Sign in to Steam

  1. Press ✕ on Steam.
  2. A Steam page opens. The stick now moves a pointer like a mouse. Move it over the username box and press ✕.
  3. A keyboard appears. Type your Steam username, then choose Done.
  4. Do the same for the password box.
  5. Move the pointer to Sign in and press ✕.
  6. If Steam asks for a code, get it from your phone or email and type it the same way.
  7. ✅ The Steam row says Signed in.
If it doesn't work: you can type with a real keyboard instead. If the page goes blank, press ○ and try again.

3.3 Sign in to Epic Games

  1. Press ✕ on Epic Games.
  2. Do the same as Steam: pointer over a box, ✕, type, Done, then Sign in.
  3. ✅ The Epic row says Signed in.

3.4 Sign in to Ubisoft Connect (the longest one, take your time)

Ubisoft does not allow the console's own browser, so this uses Chrome.

  1. Press ✕ on Ubisoft Connect in the console. A small instructions window opens on this screen, and Chrome opens the Ubisoft login.
  2. In Chrome, type your Ubisoft email and password with the real keyboard and sign in.
  3. You may land on a "page can't be found" (404) error. That is normal. Leave it.
  4. In the small window, press Open Ubisoft page again.
  5. You should see Welcome back! with a blue Continue button. Do not press Continue.
  6. First time only: make the bookmark. Copy this code: press Copy code under the box. If the button does nothing, click in the box, press Ctrl + A, then Ctrl + C.
  7. In Chrome, on the Welcome back page, press Ctrl + D. Change the name to Send to console and press Done.
  8. Right-click that bookmark (in the bar at the top, or under All Bookmarks) and choose Edit.
  9. Click in the URL box, press Ctrl + A, then Ctrl + V. Check that the URL starts with the word javascript:. If Chrome removed it, type javascript: at the very start. Press Save.
  10. Still on the Welcome back page, click the Send to console bookmark.
  11. A box says Copied with a number. Press OK.
  12. In the small window, press Continue.
  13. ✅ The console shows Signed in on the Ubisoft row.
If it doesn't work:
• "Not signed in to Ubisoft here yet": press Open Ubisoft page again, sign in on that page, then click the bookmark again.
• "Access is denied for this document": you are on an error page. Press Open Ubisoft page again and use the Welcome back page.
• The bookmark does nothing: the URL does not start with javascript:. Edit it again.
• The console says "Nothing copied yet": click the bookmark again, then press Continue.
After the first time, you do not need to make the bookmark again. It is saved: just click it.

3.5 EA app, GOG, Xbox

Nothing to sign in. Their installed games are found when you scan in Part 6. Tick this box once you have read it.

PART 4: THE FREE KEYS (covers, name art, descriptions)

These let the console find cover pictures, name art and game info for all your games. They are free. Use the keyboard and mouse for the websites.

4.1 SteamGridDB key (cover art and game name art)

  1. In Chrome, go to steamgriddb.com.
  2. Press Sign in and log in with Steam.
  3. Click your name or avatar, then open Preferences.
  4. Find the API tab and press Generate API key.
  5. Select the key and press Ctrl + C to copy it.
  6. In the console, go to Settings → Games & library.
  7. Press ✕ on SteamGridDB key (game cover art).
  8. Type the key with the on-screen keyboard, or paste it with Ctrl + V if you have a keyboard. Choose Done.
  9. ✅ The row says Set.

4.2 RAWG key (descriptions, release year, developer)

  1. In Chrome, go to rawg.io/apidocs.
  2. Press Get API Key and make a free account. Check your email if it asks you to verify.
  3. Copy the key it shows you.
  4. In the console, press ✕ on RAWG key (descriptions, year, developer).
  5. Enter the key the same way, then Done.
  6. ✅ The row says Set.

4.3 Steam API key (achievements and friends)

  1. In Chrome, go to steamcommunity.com/dev/apikey and log in.
  2. For "Domain Name" type localhost and agree to the terms.
  3. Press Register and copy the key.
  4. In the console, press ✕ on Steam API key (achievements and friends), enter the key, then Done.
  5. In Steam, open your profile, go to Edit Profile → Privacy Settings and set Game details to Public.
If a key does not work: copy the whole key with no spaces. The keys are 32 characters. If a website looks different from what is written here, send Frankie a screenshot.

PART 5: SPOTIFY (you skip this one)

5.1 Skip Spotify

The Spotify music controls and cover art need Spotify Premium, and you do not have it. So skip this part: do nothing, and just tick the box.

You can still use Spotify normally: open the Spotify tile on the home screen and it plays in its own window. Only the extra buttons and cover art in the control panel are skipped.

PART 6: FIND ALL YOUR GAMES

6.1 Scan

  1. In the console, go to Settings → Games & library.
  2. Press ✕ on Rescan games.
  3. Wait 5 to 10 minutes. Covers and name art keep loading in the background.

6.2 Check

  1. Go to the Library on the home screen and push the stick down to see all your games.
  2. ✅ Most games should have a cover picture, and when you highlight one you should see its description on the right.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

  • No covers or descriptions: check that the keys in Part 4 say Set, then run Rescan games again and wait 10 minutes.
  • A game has no picture or name art after 10 minutes: write down its name and send it to Frankie.
  • The console stops responding to the controller: press any button once. If that does not help, unplug the controller, wait 10 seconds and plug it back in.
  • A website window opens and the stick will not move the pointer: press ○ to go back and redo that step.
